# Why the animation shows approximately 28.93 m/s

The pusher is coasting after the initial pickup of the stationary plates. Native RCFORC reports exactly zero force on both pusher/plate contacts from **0.20793 to 2.66192 ms**, across **1228 samples**. Each contact's three force components is checked separately.

During binary samples inside that interval, pusher speed spans **28.930555–28.931341 m/s** while it travels a further **70.88 mm**. The front-pad mesh separates from the plates; the minimum surface gap is positive, reaching **10.04 mm left / 9.97 mm right**. This is checked using actual surface-node coordinates, not centers of mass.

The plates initially move faster than the pusher. Foam contact continues slowing them, with total axial foam force **3080.0–5109.0 N** during detachment. The pusher catches up and transfers momentum again. Constant speed here does not mean stopped motion.
