## Local inertia and weak-mode limits

Maximum selected-rail nodal added/physical mass ratio is **0.587299** (58.73%). Maximum across the entire cap is **3.23284**. The JSON includes the five worst nodes in each region with coordinates and masses.

No separate local acceptance threshold was previously adopted. These local values are disclosed without adding or relaxing a gate after viewing the results. The global 0.1% rule remains unchanged.

r_mode = sum(delta_m_i * |phi_i|^2) / sum(m_i * |phi_i|^2). With unchanged stiffness and fixed shape, omega_scaled / omega_physical = 1/sqrt(1+r_mode).

For illustration only, uniform-amplitude motion of the selected rails would give a frequency reduction of approximately **0.0716%**. The much more conservative maximum-nodal bound for a fixed mode supported there is **20.627%**. Neither is a computed eigenfrequency. A regional mean cannot rule out a mode concentrated on the small heavily scaled nodes.

Use the actual weak-mode shape to weight the added mass, or compare with a lower-scaling timestep case, before claiming negligible local inertia influence. Startup-only scaling prevents later mass growth but does not remove this initial modal perturbation. No deformation or stress convergence is inferred here.

Relative to the rejected four-interval case, combined rail mass addition falls from 28.65% to 0.1433%. This is a substantial improvement, while the reported local concentrations remain a limitation. The solver is not altered by this audit.
